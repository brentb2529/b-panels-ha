# B-Panels

A premium, fit-to-window **touch wall-panel dashboard** for Home Assistant,
delivered through HACS. Renders your HA entities as depth-styled glass tiles
built for kiosks and wall tablets.

- No separate backend, database, or cloud account — talks to HA directly over
  the websocket API using your existing login.
- Typed tiles for lights, dimmers, shades, scenes, sensors, climate, cameras,
  and the Alarmo alarm panel, plus a generic tile for any other domain.
- Light / dark / ambient-night themes; nested folder sub-panels.

After install, restart HA, add the **B-Panels** integration, and open it from
the sidebar.
